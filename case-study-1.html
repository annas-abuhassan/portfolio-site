Reducing Food Waste Through Collaborative Meal Planning

Many couples and households struggle to keep track of meals, ingredients and shopping. This often leads to duplicate purchases, forgotten cupboard items and food waste.

This case study explores how a shared meal planning application could simplify weekly planning, automatically generate shopping lists and reduce unnecessary spending by keeping everyone working from the same information.

01 · Problem statement

What was the problem?

Planning meals for the week sounds simple, but many households rely on separate notes, screenshots, text messages or memory. Common problems included:

  • Buying ingredients already in the cupboard.
  • Forgetting ingredients when shopping.
  • Repeated conversations about "What's for tea?"
  • Food expiring before it could be used.
  • No shared visibility of planned meals.

The challenge was to create a simple experience that encouraged planning without making it feel like extra work.
